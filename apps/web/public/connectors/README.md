# Connector icons

One-color brand icons for the connector catalog and for the link pills in messages (`packages/client/src/lib/link-pills.ts`, the reason for Jira's and Google Slides' here): the app draws them as a mask in the brand color (or the ink, for near-black, very light or many-colored brands). Brand colors live with the catalog in code (`CONNECTOR_BRANDS` in `packages/engine/src/connectors/listing.ts`), and every connector in the catalog has an icon (a test checks it).

Most come from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) 16.32.0 (CC0 1.0, public domain). `square.svg` is Square's own mark, the first three subpaths (the mark without the wordmark) of `Black/Square_Logo_2025_Black.svg` in the logo set at https://squareup.com/us/en/press/logo (https://assets.ctfassets.net/2d5q1td6cyxq/3xNJXOyxBSmmJ1lUafqHGJ/0d3bcea3bf9426dbf9a54f424e3d1fab/Square_Logo.zip), unchanged and scaled to the 24×24 view box. Square shows it only in black or white, which the ink gives in each theme. The others Simple Icons doesn't have are the brand's own mark, from its site or press kit, reduced to one color and fitted like a Simple Icons icon (the drawing itself, not the canvas it came on, spans the 24×24 view box on its long side and is centred on the short one): AbacatePay, Ahrefs, Amplitude, Attio, Close, Fireflies, Gamma, Granola, Himetrica, Jam, Jotform, Klaviyo, MillionSend, monday.com, Superwall, Tally and Visant Labs (the symbol of `visant-symbol.svg` from Visant Labs' own repository, its two shapes unchanged and fitted with a transform). LinkedIn's is the "in" mark of the logo in linkedin.com's own header, https://static.licdn.com/aero-v1/sc/h/8fkga714vy9b2wk5auqo5reeb, its path unchanged and scaled from 21 to 24.

A mark in its maker's colors, which a mask would flatten (PayPal's, Canva's, Outlook's), is in `../brands/`. The mailboxes' marks (Gmail, Outlook, iCloud) and colors live in `MAIL_BRANDS` in `packages/client/src/lib/mail.ts`.

Each icon is a trademark of its owner, used only to name the service.
